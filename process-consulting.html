Core Services

What an engagement looks like

Every engagement is modular — start with a single service and expand as your needs evolve.

01

Process Mapping & Gap Analysis

We document your as-is workflows, identify bottlenecks, duplication and missing controls, then deliver a prioritised gap report with recommended remediation paths.

02

Strategy Building — Hoshin Kanri & RASIC

We facilitate strategy sessions using Hoshin Kanri to cascade organisational objectives into team-level actions, and RASIC to lock in who owns what — eliminating ambiguity at every level.

03

Operational Playbooks

Lightweight, living documents that codify how your team executes key processes — onboarding, releases, incidents, reviews. Designed to be followed, not filed.

04

KPI Frameworks & Dashboards

We design measurement systems that surface the right signal at the right level — operational metrics for teams, outcome metrics for leadership — and connect them to a visible dashboard cadence.

05

Root Cause Analysis & Quality

We apply Why-Why and A3 root cause techniques to identify the true source of recurring problems, then establish quality gates and review checkpoints that embed defect prevention directly into the development lifecycle.

06

Change Management Coaching

New processes fail when people do not adopt them. We coach managers and team leads through the change curve — addressing resistance, building champions and sustaining momentum beyond the initial rollout.
